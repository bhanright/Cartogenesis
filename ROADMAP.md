# Roadmap

What is planned, and roughly in what order. No dates: a release lands when its work is done.

This file is the one place the roadmap is written. `:desktop:assembleSite` draws the "What comes
next" table on cartogenesis.com from it at build time, and `SiteAssemblyTest` fails if the page and this
table disagree, so there is nothing to keep in step by hand.

| Release | What it brings |
| --- | --- |
| 3.1.0 | More consistent rainfall on large maps, monsoon coasts, more realistic ice sheets, and softer edges between forest and open land. |
| 3.2.0 | Erosion that depends on rainfall and plant cover, rivers that start where the slope and water flow allow, and ice sheets without stripes down their sides. |
| 3.3.0 (current) | An Earth-sized planet with a real atmosphere: seasons, high-pressure cells, monsoons and trade winds that decide where rain and deserts fall; plains and continental shelves in Earth's proportions; and a new map style that looks like satellite imagery. |
| 3.x | Storms and the jet stream, and cold ocean currents that make coastal deserts. |
| 3.x | Waves that shape beaches, spits and barrier islands, and rivers that widen downstream, with meanders, braided reaches and marshes. |
| 3.x | More control over how the map is drawn, and a topographic style with contour lines. |
| 3.x | Faster generation on the graphics card. |
| 3.x | A choice of how big the planet is, and saved worlds that keep opening after an update. |
| 4.0 | The world as a sphere: a globe to turn, and a choice of map projections for the whole-world map and for exports. |
| 4.x | 3D models of your world: terrain for game engines, 3D-printable regions and globes, and cross sections through the land. |
| 4.x | Start from your own terrain: import a heightmap from Wonderdraft or any grayscale image, or paint the land yourself and let the climate, rivers and everything else generate around it. |
| 5.0 | The atlas: named land and sea features, labeled the way a printed atlas labels them. Renaming places and placing labels already work and will carry over. |
| 5.x+ | Tidal estuaries, coral reefs and atolls; then sets of map symbols, such as trees, hills and towns, to place over the map. |
